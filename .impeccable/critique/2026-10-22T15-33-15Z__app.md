#### Design Specificity Verdict

**LLM Assessment**:
While Event Pilot succeeds admirably on architectural purity, local-first privacy, and deterministic TypeScript math, its visual execution falls into the trap of "Standard SaaS Painted Amber." 

`DESIGN.md` laid out an inspiring, highly distinctive vision: an **inclusive celebration planning studio structured as a tactile Panchang & Utsav Almanac**—complete with warm papyrus grounds, stepped tab fore-edges, crisp ledger column rules, coupon-stamp validation seals, and translucent proposal overlay leaves. 

In practice, the live UI renders as a predictable series of white `rounded-lg border border-neutral-border p-6 shadow-card` boxes placed on an off-white background. The navigation bar (`WorkspaceNavigation.tsx`) is a conventional horizontal strip with a generic 2px amber bottom border. The tables are generic HTML grids without ledger rulings or tactile editorial warmth. The seating planner displays dry chip lists instead of evocative table silhouettes. Crucially, the entire interface is static and rigid: zero spatial transitions, no graceful stagger animations, no micro-interactions on checking off tasks or stamping deposits, and no celebration energy. It feels like an administrative corporate tool rather than a joyful, auspicious planning sanctuary.

#### Overall Impression
Event Pilot possesses world-class functional bones—186 passing unit tests, rigorous deterministic arithmetic, robust local storage, and human-in-the-loop AI safety. However, the visual presentation lacks soul, tactile craftsmanship, and emotional resonance. The single biggest opportunity is to elevate the interface from a flat utility into a **living, tactile editorial studio**: giving it genuine almanac fore-edge stepped navigation, rhythmic ledger typography with authentic folio dividers, rich micro-interactions (stamps, check-off ripples, smooth tab transitions), and an evocative seating atelier.

#### What's Working
1. **Deterministic Information Integrity**: The monetary totals, attendance roll-ups, seat counts, and budget variances update with absolute certainty. The financial indicators (budget cap, allocated, recorded actuals, unallocated buffer) never drift or flicker.
2. **AI Staging & Proposal Safety**: The AI Assistant strictly stages proposals with clear diff inspection and explicit "Accept & Apply" or "Dismiss" actions, respecting user authority and privacy.
3. **Typography Foundations**: The combination of `Eczar` (editorial serif) and `Plus Jakarta Sans` is well-chosen and gives event titles a dignified presence when given room to breathe.

#### Priority Issues

- **[P1] Visual Blandness: Generic SaaS Cards Instead of Tactile Almanac Craft**
  - **Why it matters**: Indian milestone celebrations (vivahas, milestone birthdays, utsavs) are emotionally charged and visually vibrant. Presenting them inside sterile white rectangles drains the joy and excitement from planning, making the product feel like corporate invoicing software.
  - **Fix**: Redesign the surface language around authentic almanac editorial craft: warm layered parchment tones, subtle deckle/folio hairlines, authentic stepped tab fore-edge navigation, distinct gold/terracotta ledger bands, and refined typography hierarchy.
  - **Suggested command**: `$impeccable bolder`

- **[P1] Total Lack of Motion, Choreography, and Micro-Interaction Delight**
  - **Why it matters**: Switching tabs causes a jarring instant repaint; completing tasks or marking expenses paid feels anticlimactic; opening dialogs lacks spatial presence. High-stakes planning requires reassuring, polished feedback.
  - **Fix**: Introduce purposeful, smooth motion: layout-animated tab indicators, gentle staggered card transitions, tactile stamp impression animations when marking deposits paid or RSVPs confirmed, and smooth drawer/dialog choreography with spring physics.
  - **Suggested command**: `$impeccable animate`

- **[P1] High Cognitive Load & Metric Fragmentation in Overview & Budget Tabs**
  - **Why it matters**: The Overview tab bombards the user with 15+ disconnected numbers across 10 nested cards, violating the working memory limit (≤4 items). Users experience decision paralysis instead of immediate clarity on what needs attention.
  - **Fix**: Consolidate metrics into a cohesive "Executive Almanac Ledger": a clear primary status dial/countdown, a unified financial summary bar with progressive disclosure, and an actionable "Next Milestone & Urgent Tasks" focus card.
  - **Suggested command**: `$impeccable layout`

- **[P2] Dry, Abstract Seating Planner Lacking Spatial Metaphor**
  - **Why it matters**: Hosts plan seating spatially—picturing round banquet tables, rectangular head tables, stage orientation, and family clusters. Viewing seating as just a progress bar and text tags makes table arrangement tedious and unnatural.
  - **Fix**: Elevate the seating planner into an interactive table atelier: visually render round and rectangular table silhouettes with seated chair dots, dietary indicators, capacity rings, and intuitive assignment controls.
  - **Suggested command**: `$impeccable delight`

- **[P2] Monolithic Event Creation Form**
  - **Why it matters**: The initial creation dialog forces users to fill in 8+ fields at once on a long scrolling modal, creating friction at the very first step of their journey.
  - **Fix**: Transform event creation into an inspiring, guided 3-step ritual (1: Celebration Identity & Archetype, 2: Date & Location, 3: Budget & Guest Scale) with smart defaults and celebratory animation.
  - **Suggested command**: `$impeccable onboard`
