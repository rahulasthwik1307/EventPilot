## Design Specificity Verdict

The transformation across Event Pilot has firmly realized the "Panchang & Utsav Almanac" identity specified in [DESIGN.md](file:///e:/EventPilot/DESIGN.md):
- **Welcome Hero & Archetype Cards**: The landing experience has shed generic AI SaaS conventions. The headline stands in stately upright Roman Eczar with an integrated cultural kicker. The archetype cards feature auspicious top hairline accents, invitation-grade typography, and clear benefits.

- **Keyboard Navigation**: Pressing `1` through `7` switches workspaces instantly, and pressing `?` reveals a dedicated shortcuts cheatsheet.

- **What**: Modifying expenses or deleting guests currently relies on immediate local storage updates without a quick "Undo" toast.
- **Why it matters**: Accidental clicks require manual re-entry.
- **Fix**: Introduce a 5-second undo toast for destructive operations.
- **Suggested command**: `$impeccable harden`

- **What**: Milestones are currently viewed only within the browser.
- **Why it matters**: Planners benefit from syncing auspicious Muhurtams to family calendar apps.
- **Fix**: Add a one-click "Export to .ics" button for milestones.
