# Surface Brief: Event Pilot Application Workspace

## Scope & Mode
- **Scope:** Root application workspace (`app`), covering the Event Setup Wizard and the Main Planning Dashboard (Budget, Timeline, Vendors, Guests, Seating, and AI Staged Proposal Drawer).
- **Mode:** `operate` (high-scanability, task completion, accurate data density, reliable feedback) with an initial `persuade` intake gate for event creation.

## Audience & Job
- **Audience:** Indian couples, families, and community coordinators planning high-stakes milestone events (weddings, milestone birthdays, cultural/neighborhood festivals).
- **Job:** Turn complex, multi-variable celebration requirements into a verified, costed, and scheduled execution plan with deterministic certainty and AI acceleration.

## Direction contract

THESIS: An auspicious celebration planning studio designed as a chronological almanac (Panchang), refusing the sterile coldness of generic SaaS tables and the chaotic frivolousness of bridal scrapbooks.

OWN-WORLD: Warm papyrus and ivory grounds (`#FDFBF7`, `#F5EFEB`), deep charcoal-ink typography (`#1C1917`), saffron and marigold accent hues (`#D97706`, `#B45309`), restrained terracotta borders (`#C2410C`), stepped tab fore-edge navigation, coupon-stamp validation badges, and translucent overlay leaves for previewing AI proposal diffs without losing place.

STORY: The user lands on a calm, ordered sanctuary where high-stakes celebration chaos resolves into structured certainty. They review deterministic budgets that balance down to the rupee, verify chronological timeline countdowns, inspect vendor checklists, and test AI ideas via staged visual diffs that never overwrite their authority.

FIRST VIEWPORT: A wide two-tier header anchor with event metadata (Event Name, Date Countdown, Confirmed Guest Count, Total Budget remaining vs spent). A stepped lateral tab rail down the left fore-edge (`Overview`, `Budget Ledger`, `Timeline`, `Vendors`, `Guests & Seating`, `AI Assistant`). The central canvas displays the active workspace with structured data cards and a persistent bottom action bar for instant actions and demo resetting.
